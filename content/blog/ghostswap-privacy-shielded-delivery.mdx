Every cross-chain swap already delivers to whatever address you specify, from a solver's own inventory rather than a direct wallet-to-wallet transfer. GhostSwap is that same real mechanism, paired with a one-click way to generate a brand-new destination address you've never used before.

## How it works

Pick your source token and chain as normal, then on the destination side, click "Generate a fresh, unlinked wallet" instead of pasting an address. A real keypair is generated locally in your browser (Solana or EVM, matching your destination chain) — the address is shown immediately, and the private key once, with a checkbox confirming you've saved it before it's usable. Once confirmed, your swap delivers there like any other cross-chain swap.

## What this actually protects against

Block explorers and analytics tools build wallet-linkage graphs primarily off direct transfers between addresses. A swap that lands funds on a brand-new address, sourced from a solver's inventory rather than a direct hop from your main wallet, doesn't create that obvious edge in the graph. It's genuinely useful for keeping a fresh position from being immediately associated with a wallet you're known to hold — but it's not a substitute for real privacy tooling if that's your actual threat model. Sophisticated analysis can still correlate transactions by timing and amount even without a direct on-chain link.
