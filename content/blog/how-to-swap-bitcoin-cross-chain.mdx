## Why BTC works differently from a Solana/EVM swap

Every other swap on this app is wallet-to-wallet: connect a wallet, sign a transaction, done. Bitcoin doesn't have the same smart-contract signing model Solana and EVM chains do, so a BTC-sourced swap works the way real, non-custodial BTC exchanges always have — you get a deposit address specific to your swap request, send BTC to it from your own wallet, and the destination asset is delivered once that deposit is confirmed on-chain. It's slower than a same-ecosystem swap (Bitcoin's own confirmation times apply), but it's still non-custodial: nothing sits in a Blockchains.Click-controlled wallet at any point.

## What executes it

BTC swaps run through ChangeNOW, a real, established exchange engine — not the Jupiter/Relay pipeline the rest of this app uses for Solana/EVM swaps. Same principle as every engine choice on this app: BTC doesn't have a comparable on-chain aggregator the way Solana and EVM do, so a real, live-quoted third-party exchange is the honest way to support it rather than fabricating a bridge that doesn't exist.
