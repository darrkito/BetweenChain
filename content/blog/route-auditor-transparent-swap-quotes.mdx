Most cross-chain swap interfaces show you a single number and ask you to trust it. Route Auditor shows the real numbers behind it.

## What it shows

Click "Route details" on any swap preview and see exactly what both routing engines already know about your specific trade: the real price impact percentage (broken out by Relay and Jupiter's own impact calculations), a real estimated time to completion in seconds, and which actual DEX or liquidity pool is filling each leg of the route — not a generic "via DEX aggregator" label, the real name.

## Why no confidence score

A single number like "99.4% confidence" sounds precise, but neither Relay nor Jupiter's public APIs expose anything resembling a calculated confidence metric — inventing one would just be marketing dressed up as data. The real, honest version of this feature is simpler: show the actual price impact and actual time estimate, and let you judge for yourself.
