Most NFT projects that started as a 2022-era PFP collection are either dead or coasting on nostalgia by now. Claynosaurz did neither — it's arguably one of the few Web3-native IPs that's actually broken into mainstream entertainment on its own terms.

## From Solana mint to streaming service

In July 2026, Claynosaurz's animated micro-episode series launched on **Amazon Prime Video and Apple TV** — a 39-episode run built around the characters and world that started as on-chain PFP art. That's not a licensing deal where a studio bought the rights and made something disconnected from the community; the show is explicitly part of the same ecosystem the NFT holders are already inside of, including tie-ins to the trading card packs described below.

## The equity move

On July 2, 2026, the team behind Claynosaurz reserved **15% of company equity** specifically for NFT holders. This is a genuinely unusual move for the space — most NFT "utility" promises stay inside the token/NFT layer (governance votes, staking yield, allowlist spots). Real equity in the underlying company is a different category of commitment, and it's the kind of thing that only makes sense if the team is planning for this to be a durable, multi-decade brand rather than a cycle-driven flip.

## What is HEEBOO?

**HEEBOO** is the entertainment launchpad studio Claynosaurz operates under — not a governance token for the NFT collection itself, but a separate fan-participation platform anchored by the **$HEEBOO token**. The pitch: fans who discover and support entertainment IP early should get real, ongoing recognition for it, not just first-mover bragging rights.

So far, **25 million $HEEBOO tokens (2.5% of total supply)** have already been distributed, allocated to Claynosaurz holders based on their holding and staking activity. The **Pioneer Edition** promo trading-card packs — tied to the Prime Video micro-episodes, mint closed July 28, 2026 with a hard cap of 200,000 cards — each included a random $HEEBOO allocation of 50 to 10,000 tokens per pack when opened.

## The family is growing

Most recently, **Nobody Sausage** — one of the most recognizable character brands on the internet, with over 33 million followers across platforms — officially joined the HEEBOO family. It's the clearest signal yet that HEEBOO's model (bring an already-massive audience into an on-chain, fan-owned structure) is working well enough to attract IP that never needed Web3 in the first place.
