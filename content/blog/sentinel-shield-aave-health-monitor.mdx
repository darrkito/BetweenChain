Checking a leveraged position's health factor usually means opening Aave's own dashboard, or several, if you're borrowing on more than one chain. Sentinel Shield puts it in one place.

## What it shows

Connect an EVM wallet and Sentinel Shield reads your real collateral, debt, and health factor directly from Aave's own Pool contract on each supported chain — no third-party dashboard, no delay. A health factor under 1.0 means the position can be liquidated; this page flags anything approaching that line before it gets there.

## Why this stops at monitoring

The natural next step — automatically topping up collateral or repaying debt when a threshold is crossed — is real, but it's also the single highest-consequence kind of automation this app could ship: a bug either direction costs real money, either by failing to protect a position in time or by moving funds incorrectly. That deserves its own dedicated reliability review, not a bolt-on to a monitoring dashboard. This ships the part that's unambiguously useful with none of that risk: a clear, live number.
