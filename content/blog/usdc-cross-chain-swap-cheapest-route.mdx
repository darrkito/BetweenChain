\"Cheapest way to move USDC across chains\" doesn't have one answer, because \"moving USDC\" and \"swapping into something else on another chain\" are two different jobs — and the cheapest tool for one is usually the wrong tool for the other.

## If you just need USDC on a different chain: CCTP wins

**Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP)** is Circle's own native mechanism: it burns USDC on the source chain and mints freshly-issued USDC on the destination chain. No wrapped token, no liquidity pool, no protocol fee — the only cost is gas on both ends, typically **$0.05 to $0.50 total** at 2026 gas prices. For a pure USDC-to-USDC move under roughly $5,000, this is usually the cheapest path that exists, because there's no fee layered on top of gas at all.

The catch: CCTP moves USDC to USDC. It doesn't convert to anything else. If your actual goal is "I have USDC on Ethereum and I need SOL on Solana," CCTP gets you USDC on Solana — you'd still need a second, separate swap once it lands.

## Mid-size routed transfers: Across and Stargate

For **$1,000–$10,000** transfers, especially between EVM L2s, **Across** charges roughly a **0.04% relayer fee** plus a small base fee, with settlement typically completing in under 30 seconds — the percentage cost stays small in absolute terms at this size. **Stargate** uses unified liquidity pools across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Polygon, and more, charging around **0.06%** per cross-chain swap. Both are purpose-built stablecoin/EVM routing tools, and both are typically cheaper than a general-purpose swap aggregator for a same-asset move at this size.

## When you actually need a swap, not just a move

None of the above convert USDC into a different asset. If the real goal is **USDC on Ethereum → SOL on Solana**, or **USDC → BTC**, or any case where the destination token isn't USDC itself, a routed swap is the correct tool — and that's a different job than a same-asset bridge, so it's priced differently too.

On this platform, that's a flat **0.25% per leg** via Jupiter (Solana-side) and Relay (cross-chain delivery) — a same-chain swap, or a cross-chain swap starting from native SOL, is one leg; converting an SPL token like USDC into SOL first is a second leg (0.5% total). The rate doesn't move with route selection, unlike some aggregators — see [how the fee structure compares to Jumper and deBridge](/blog/blockchains-click-vs-jumper-vs-debridge) for the full breakdown against other swap-specific options.
