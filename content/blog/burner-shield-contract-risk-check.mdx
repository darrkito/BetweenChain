## What it checks

Paste any contract or token address and Burner Shield queries two real signals: whether the address itself has any history of malicious activity (phishing, theft, sanctions, mixer association), and — for tokens specifically — whether it's a honeypot, has abnormal buy/sell tax, or gives the contract owner the ability to directly change balances. All from GoPlus Security's live public data, not a static list.

## Why this instead of a full isolated-execution sandbox

The original idea behind a feature like this was a one-time, disposable smart wallet for every risky interaction — real, but it needs account-abstraction infrastructure this app has never used before, plus a decision about funding gas for contracts nobody has vetted in advance. That's a meaningfully bigger commitment than a pre-sign check, for the same underlying goal: catching a bad interaction before it costs you anything. This ships the check now; the full sandbox stays a real, scoped idea for later.
