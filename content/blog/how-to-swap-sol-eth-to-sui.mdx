## Why a SUI destination works differently from a Solana/EVM swap

Every SOL-to-ETH swap on this app is wallet-to-wallet: connect a wallet, sign a transaction, done. Sui isn't reachable through that same Jupiter/Relay pipeline yet, so a SUI-destination swap works the way real BTC/SUI exchanges do — you get a deposit address specific to your swap request, send SOL or ETH to it from your own wallet, and native SUI is delivered once that deposit confirms on-chain. It's genuinely custodial for the brief window in between (your deposit sits with ChangeNOW, not a smart contract, until it delivers), the same trade-off [swapping into Bitcoin](/blog/how-to-swap-bitcoin-cross-chain) already makes on this app — worth being upfront about rather than implied otherwise.

## What executes it

SOL/ETH-to-SUI swaps run through ChangeNOW, the same real, established exchange engine that handles this app's Bitcoin swaps — not the Jupiter/Relay pipeline used for Solana/EVM pairs. Sui doesn't have a comparable on-chain bridge aggregator the way Solana and EVM do, so a real, live-quoted third-party exchange is the honest way to support it.
