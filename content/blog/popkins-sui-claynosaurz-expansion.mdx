Claynosaurz's core collection lives on Solana. Popkins is a deliberate, separate expansion onto **Sui** — and instead of just porting the same PFP-mint format to a new chain, the team built a genuinely different mechanic around it.

## The mystery-pack mint

Instead of a straightforward "pay X, get a guaranteed NFT" mint, Popkins sold **25,000 mystery packs at 200 USDC each** — but only **2,000 of those packs** actually contained a Popkin NFT. Everyone else wasn't left with nothing: non-winning pack buyers were refunded and given additional rewards on top, so the downside of "not winning" was cushioned rather than a straight loss.

The packs themselves came in three tiers — **Escape Packs**, **Legendary Packs**, and **Rat Packs** — each with different odds and different digital rewards bundled in regardless of whether you actually pulled a Popkin. The whole thing ties into a future Web3 mobile game the Claynosaurz team has planned, where Popkins holders get deeper in-game integration than non-holders.

## Why the numbers matter

**97,347 NFT tickets minted** against a 25,000-pack cap tells you the mint mechanic itself became a repeat-engagement loop, not a one-and-done purchase — a meaningful share of participants came back for more than one pack. **17,760 unique holders** for a 2,000-NFT-supply collection also tells you the reward/refund structure did its job: most participants who didn't pull a Popkin still stuck around as part of the ecosystem instead of just walking away.

## Why did Claynosaurz choose Sui for the Popkins expansion?

Sui's transaction model is built for exactly this kind of high-throughput, bursty mint event — thousands of pack purchases landing in a tight window without the network choking or fees spiking unpredictably. That's a real, practical reason to pick a second chain for an expansion like this rather than just cloning the original Solana mint format.

## The part that actually matters if you want in

Popkins is a **real, live, tradeable Sui collection** — not a wrapped or synthetic version. It's already listed on this platform's own marketplace, and the same rule that applies to every collection here applies to Popkins too: you don't need a Sui wallet stocked with SUI ahead of time. Pay from Solana or an EVM wallet, and the cross-chain conversion happens as part of the purchase itself.
