Every dollar of swap volume you trade on Blockchains.Click earns you points — no separate opt-in, no staking requirement, it's tracked automatically against your wallet as you trade.

## The referral split

Refer a friend and, once they sign up with your invite, both of you get a bonus automatically:

- **You get 20%** of their swap volume as bonus points.
- **They get 10%** of their own swap volume as a welcome bonus.

Both apply on an ongoing basis, not just their first trade — as long as they keep swapping through your invite link, you keep earning a share.

## Why we built it this way

A flat percentage-of-volume model means the incentive scales naturally: refer someone who trades a little, you get a little; refer someone who trades a lot, you get a lot. There's no cap to game and no tiered structure to game around.

## How points get credited

Points are credited server-side, only once a swap has reached its final confirmed state — never based on a client-reported value, and never double-counted even if you (or a bug) fires the same confirmation twice. If a swap doesn't go through, no points are credited for it.

Points and referral tracking are tied to your connected wallet — connect on [the swap page](/swap) to see your current balance and invite link. Have questions about mechanics we haven't covered here? Check [our FAQ](/faq).
