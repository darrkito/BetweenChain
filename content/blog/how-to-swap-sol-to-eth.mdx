Swapping SOL for ETH doesn't require a centralized exchange, a manual bridge, or a second "unwrap" transaction on the other side. Blockchains.Click routes it as one flow: pick your tokens, review a real quote, sign once.

Native SOL never touches Jupiter — Jupiter's conversion leg only activates when you're selling a different Solana token (not SOL itself). For the default SOL → ETH pair, Relay is the only engine involved, which is also why the fee is a flat 0.25% here instead of the 0.25%-per-leg figure a non-native token would carry.

## Connect Your Wallets

Open the Blockchains.Click header and connect your Solana wallet. If you want ETH delivered to your own wallet, connect an EVM wallet too — otherwise you can paste any destination address instead. The destination address is locked in the moment you get a quote and can't be changed afterward, which closes off address-swap attacks on this step.

<BlogTip>
Double-check the destination address before confirming, especially if you're sending to an exchange deposit address rather than your own wallet — some exchanges require a specific network tag that a personal wallet doesn't.
</BlogTip>
