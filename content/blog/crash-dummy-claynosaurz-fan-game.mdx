If you've spent any time in the Claynosaurz community on X, you've probably already seen **Crash Dummy** — a free browser game where you launch a dummy character and chase the distance record, built entirely by a community member rather than the official Claynosaurz team. It's the kind of fan project that only happens around an NFT collection with a genuinely engaged community behind it, and Claynosaurz has one of the more active ones in the space.

## What is Crash Dummy?

Crash Dummy is a launcher-style physics arcade game — pick your angle and power, launch, and try to glide as far as possible before you crash. It's simple to pick up, genuinely fun to chase a new personal best in, and entirely free to play with no wallet connection required.

</Callout>

## Who made it

Crash Dummy was built by **@Degen_Bald_Boy**, a member of the Claynosaurz community — not the official Claynosaurz team itself. It's a genuine fan project, the kind of thing that tends to show up organically around collections whose holders actually stick around and build things rather than just flip and leave. That community energy is a big part of what makes Claynosaurz worth paying attention to beyond the art itself.

## Why this matters beyond one game

A community building a real, playable game around an NFT collection — for free, with no token incentive attached — is a genuine signal of engagement that's rarer than it should be in this space. Whether or not you hold a Claynosaurz, Saga, or Popkins piece, Crash Dummy is worth a few minutes just to see what a community builds when it actually cares about the world it's part of.
