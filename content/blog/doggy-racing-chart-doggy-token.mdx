## How it works

Every run generates its bike terrain from a real price chart, so the game is quite literally different depending on what the market is doing that day. Pump hard and you're riding a steep climb; dump hard and you're dropping into a crash you have to survive. It's motocross built on top of live crypto data, not a static hand-built level.
